### Prev: expt_b100 (base)

Cell 24 of the 2^5 workload-B ablation (`ablation/README.md`); every cell's factor levels are in `ablation/manifest_b.csv`. Generated by `ablation/generate.py --side b` from `ablation/template/`: change the template and regenerate rather than editing this directory.

Every cell of the workload-B ablation is generated; none is a renamed earlier experiment. `expt_b100` is `expt_b3`'s code on the A-ablation template. `transactions/` and `explain/` are byte-identical to `expt_a124`'s, the workload-A cell with the same mask; `run.sh` differs from it only in the node pin (`xcnf[17-21]`), the mail user and the `TEAM_ID` and `WORKLOAD` defaults.

Everything not listed below is exactly `expt_b100`'s: schema, distribution, indexes, T2, T3, T4, T8, T9 and server parameters. `run.sh` also records plans outside the timed benchmark, as `expt_a115` does.

Enabled factors:

| Bit (value) | Factor | Source |
| ----------- | ------ | ------ |
| 3 (8) | T1 delegation: T1 is the `place_order` distributed function, called outside a transaction block; `run.sh` creates and distributes the function | `expt_b7` |
| 4 (16) | Repeatable read: `PG_ISOLATION_LEVEL` defaults to `repeatable read` instead of `serializable` | `origin/tl` `expt_a7` (later also `expt_b14` on branch `b-side`) |

T1's `place_order` is `expt_b7`'s with steps 6 (`merchant_daily_sales` upsert) and 7 (`order_merchant_map` insert) removed from the function body. The signature, the pgbench-side statements and the rest of the body are unchanged, and the remaining steps keep `expt_b7`'s numbering.
Step 4 still has `RETURNING total_amount, created_at INTO v_order_total, v_created_at`, although only step 6 read them.

The default transaction isolation level on every node is `repeatable read`, not `serializable`. The teammate's argument for why the transactions stay correct under it, and its limits, is in `git show origin/tl:expt_a7/optimisations.md`.
