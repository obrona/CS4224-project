### What is switched off

- **T6 rollup.** T6 is `expt_b8`'s: it aggregates the 30-day window from `orders`. There is no `merchant_daily_sales` or `idx_mds_period`: `run.sh` does not create, distribute, fill or truncate them, and T1 does not upsert the rollup.
- **T7 routing.** T7 is `expt_b8`'s: six statements by `product_id`/`inventory_id`, each sent to every `inventory` shard. There is no `product_merchant_map`, and the coordinator is not registered in Citus's metadata (it was only needed for that reference table).

T1 is `expt_b19`'s `place_order` with step 6 (rollup upsert) removed from the function body; nothing else in the file changes.
