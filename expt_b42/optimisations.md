### What this tests

`expt_b13` (32 shards), `expt_b14` (8) and `expt_b15` (4) swept the shard count before T7 was routed through the reference table (`expt_b17` .. `expt_b19`). `expt_b42` .. `expt_b46` repeat the sweep on the final design, with 1 and 2 shards added. The shard count applies to every distributed table, including `order_merchant_map`.

- **1 shard.** Every merchant-colocated table is a single shard, so they all sit on one worker: that worker runs every merchant-scoped transaction, and T6 is one task. The other three workers hold none of those tables.
